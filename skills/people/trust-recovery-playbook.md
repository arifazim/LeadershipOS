# Skill: Trust Recovery Playbook

**Domain**: People Leadership & Organizational Health
**Owner**: `subagents/engineering-coach.md`
**Cadence**: Post-Crisis (Reorg, Layoff, Project Failure) + Monthly Follow-up

---

## Purpose

Accelerate the rebuilding of trust after a high-stress organizational event (reorg, layoff, failed pivot). Trust rebuild cannot be automated, but it can be *systematized* to ensure consistent behavior, transparency, and psychological safety. This playbook replaces "hope" with a structured 6-month recovery path.

---

## The Trust Recovery Phases

### Phase 1: Acknowledgment & Stability (Month 1)
- **Goal**: Stop the bleeding; acknowledge the reality without spin.
- **Action**: "The Vulnerability Session." EM acknowledges what they know, what they don't, and the emotional impact on the team.
- **Metric**: Attendance and engagement in "Ask Me Anything" sessions.

### Phase 2: Radical Transparency (Months 2-3)
- **Goal**: Re-establish the information flow.
- **Action**: "The Decision Open-Door." Share the [Decision Provenance](../../subagents/decision-provenance-agent.md) for all new org-level changes.
- **Metric**: % of team who report "understanding the 'why' behind decisions."

### Phase 3: Small Wins & Predictability (Months 4-6)
- **Goal**: Prove consistency through action.
- **Action**: "Commitment Tracking." EM tracks their own commitments to the team publicly and hits them 100%.
- **Metric**: Team Confidence Score in the [Master Dashboard](../../analytics/master-leadership-dashboard.md).

---

## Signals of Trust Decay vs. Recovery

| Signal | Decay (Red) | Recovery (Green) |
|---|---|---|
| **Communication** | Silence in meetings; back-channeling. | Direct questions; healthy dissent. |
| **Risk-Taking** | extreme caution; fear of failure. | Calculated risks; "Learner Safety" present. |
| **Accountability** | Blame-shifting; "Not my job." | Ownership; blameless post-mortems. |
| **Retention** | High attrition of high performers. | Stable retention; referrals resuming. |

---

## Decision Tree for EM

```markdown
Is there a significant trust event (Reorg/Layoff)?
├── YES
│   ├── Have we acknowledged the impact? (Phase 1)
│   │   ├── NO → Schedule team-wide AMA; admit uncertainty.
│   │   └── YES → Move to Phase 2 (Transparency).
│   ├── Are we sharing the 'why' behind all changes?
│   │   ├── NO → Start publishing Decision Provenance logs.
│   │   └── YES → Move to Phase 3 (Predictability).
│   └── Is the team confidence score rising?
│       ├── NO → Identify the 'Trust Tax' (unmet commitments) and fix.
│       └── YES → Maintain cadence; resume long-term growth plans.
└── NO → Maintain standard Leadership Health rituals.
```
