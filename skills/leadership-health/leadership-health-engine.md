# Skill: Leadership Health Engine

**Domain**: Leadership & Management Capability
**Owner**: `subagents/engineering-manager.md`
**Cadence**: Quarterly Assessment + Continuous Signal Monitoring

---

## Purpose

A unified diagnostic engine to measure, track, and improve engineering leadership capability across 13 core dimensions. This engine replaces fragmented assessments with a holistic view of trust, influence, execution, and team development.

This skill answers:
1. What is the overall Leadership Health Score (0-100)?
2. Which specific leadership pillars are strong vs. at-risk?
3. What systemic patterns (Micromanagement, Trust Deficit, Clarity Gap) are emerging?

---

## The 13 Dimensions of Leadership Health

### Pillar 1: Trust & Relationships
- **Executive Trust**: Confidence level from senior leadership.
- **Stakeholder Alignment**: Quality of cross-functional partnerships.
- **Organizational Clarity**: How well the team understands their roles and the org structure.
- **Political Awareness**: Ability to navigate organizational dynamics ethically.

### Pillar 2: Communication & Influence
- **Communication Effectiveness**: Clarity, brevity, and impact of communication.
- **Influence Score**: Ability to drive change without formal authority.
- **Meeting Quality**: Effectiveness and necessity of meetings led by the EM.

### Pillar 3: Execution & Ownership
- **Delegation Score**: Distribution of decision-making authority vs. EM bottlenecking.
- **Decision Quality**: Soundness and durability of leadership decisions.
- **Ownership Index**: Team's proactive accountability for outcomes.
- **Execution Clarity**: Clarity of priorities and progress tracking.

### Pillar 4: Team Development
- **Coaching Score**: Impact of 1:1s and individual growth conversations.
- **Team Autonomy**: Ability of the team to operate without EM intervention.

---

## Scoring & Pattern Recognition

### Holistic Score Calculation
Apply the following weights (summing to 100%):
- **Executive Trust / Stakeholder Alignment**: 12.7% each
- **Organizational Clarity / Communication / Decision Quality / Coaching / Delegation**: ~7.6% - 9.6% each
- **Influence / Ownership / Autonomy / Political Awareness / Meeting Quality / Execution**: ~4.5% - 6.4% each

**Score Thresholds**:
- **80–100 (Green)**: Strong leadership. Focus on mastery.
- **60–79 (Yellow)**: Functional with localized gaps.
- **40–59 (At Risk)**: Multiple underperforming dimensions. Structured intervention required.
- **0–39 (Red)**: Leadership crisis. Immediate role adjustment or intensive coaching needed.

### Systemic Pattern Detection
- **Micromanagement**: Low Delegation + Low Autonomy. (EM overrides decisions)
- **Trust Deficit**: Low Exec Trust + Low Stakeholder Alignment. (Relationship strain)
- **Clarity Gap**: Low Org Clarity + Low Execution Clarity. (Roles and priorities confused)
- **Talk-Only Leadership**: High Communication + Low Decision Quality + Low Execution.
- **Isolated Contributor**: Low Influence + Low Political Awareness + Low Alignment.
- **Burnout Precursor**: Low Coaching + Low Ownership + Low Meeting Quality.
