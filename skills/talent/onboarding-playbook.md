# Skill: Onboarding Playbook

**Domain**: Talent Acquisition & Team Health
**Owner**: `subagents/talent-partner.md`
**Cadence**: Per new hire (First 90 days)

---

## Purpose

Design the first 90 days for success. A structured onboarding plan reduces time-to-productivity, increases new hire confidence, and improves long-term retention.

---

## The 30-60-90 Day Framework

### 30 Days: Integration & Context
- **Goal**: Understand the team, the tech, and the product.
- **Milestones**: First commit, complete onboarding tasks, meet all team members.
- **Support**: Assign an Onboarding Buddy (peer) and a Technical Mentor.

### 60 Days: Contribution & Ownership
- **Goal**: Deliver a small, independent feature or bug fix.
- **Milestones**: Lead a small technical discussion, participate in code reviews, contribute to documentation.
- **Feedback**: First 60-day check-in with EM.

### 90 Days: Impact & Autonomy
- **Goal**: Fully own a project or significant domain area.
- **Milestones**: Propose a technical improvement, mentor a peer on a specific topic, participate in an on-call rotation (if applicable).
- **Outcome**: Successful completion of onboarding phase.

---

## Analysis: Success Signals vs. Risk Signals

### Success Signals
- New hire asks clarifying questions about the "why," not just the "how."
- New hire identifies a documentation gap and fixes it.
- Peer feedback is positive regarding collaboration and curiosity.

### Risk Signals
- New hire is silent in meetings for 2+ weeks.
- Time-to-first-commit exceeds 10 days.
- New hire expresses frustration with toolchain or environment without seeking help.
