# Skill: Interview Calibration

**Domain**: Talent Acquisition
**Owner**: `subagents/talent-partner.md`
**Cadence**: Before every new interview loop + After every 5 candidates

---

## Purpose

Ensure a consistent, unbiased hiring bar across the interview team. Calibration prevents "bar drift" where different interviewers apply different standards, and it mitigates common hiring biases (affinity bias, halo effect, etc.).

---

## Analysis

### Step 1: Scorecard Design
- **Core Competencies**: Technical depth, system design, collaboration, leadership.
- **Evidence-Based Rating**: Scores must be backed by specific observations, not "gut feel."
- **Standardized Rubric**: What does "Exceeds Bar" look like for a Senior Engineer vs. a Staff Engineer?

### Step 2: Bias Mitigation
- **Affinity Bias**: "They went to my school/worked at my old company."
- **Halo/Horn Effect**: One great (or bad) answer coloring the entire interview.
- **Confirmation Bias**: Looking for evidence to support an initial impression.
- **Protocol**: Review feedback for "culture fit" language (replace with "value alignment").

### Step 3: Calibration Session
- **Roundtable**: Review feedback from the full loop.
- **Discrepancy Resolution**: If one interviewer says "Strong Hire" and another says "No Hire," dig into the specific evidence provided by both.
- **Final Decision**: The EM makes the call based on the calibrated feedback.
