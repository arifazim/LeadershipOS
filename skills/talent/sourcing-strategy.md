# Skill: Sourcing Strategy

**Domain**: Talent Acquisition
**Owner**: `subagents/talent-partner.md`
**Cadence**: Per open role + Weekly pipeline review

---

## Purpose

Attract high-quality, diverse engineering talent by defining where and how to look. This skill moves beyond "post and pray" to a proactive, multi-channel sourcing strategy that targets specific skill sets and organizational needs.

---

## Inputs

### Required
- **Role Profile**: Level, primary tech stack, and key impact areas.
- **Diversity Goals**: Specific focus areas for pipeline representation.
- **Urgency**: Target start date.

### Optional
- Current funnel metrics.
- Competitive landscape (e.g., companies with similar tech stacks).

---

## Analysis

### Step 1: Channel Selection
- **Outbound**: LinkedIn, GitHub, technical communities (e.g., Slack groups, Discord).
- **Referrals**: Internal team networks (high conversion, but watch for "mini-me" bias).
- **Inbound**: Job boards, careers page, niche tech boards (e.g., We Work Remotely).
- **Events**: Meetups, conferences, hackathons.

### Step 2: Messaging & Outreach
- **The Hook**: Why this role, this team, and this problem? (Avoid generic recruiter templates).
- **Transparency**: Include comp range, tech stack details, and remote/hybrid policy upfront.
- **Personalization**: Reference the candidate's specific work or open-source contributions.

### Step 3: Diversity & Inclusion
- **Bias Mitigation**: Use gender-neutral language in job descriptions.
- **Targeted Sourcing**: Identify organizations or communities that support underrepresented groups in tech.
- **Funnel Analysis**: Track where underrepresented candidates drop off.
